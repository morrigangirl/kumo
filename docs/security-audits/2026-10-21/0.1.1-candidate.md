Objective-C catch/finally, Rust catch/Drop, backtraces through Chromium's actual

focused check does not prove every engine ABI path or arm64e/PAC behavior.

## Preserved signed-stage failure and replacement

The Developer ID-signed build `202610200002` passed its PhishLang, domain-blocking,
extension-seal and update-status checks, and all six normal native-download cases.
Three CDP runs nevertheless cancelled a benign download. A metadata trace showed
the live intermediate file's ctime changing during scanning while inode, byte
count, ownership and modification time remained fixed. The strict original-file
postcheck therefore had a sufficient rejection condition. The xattr trace did not
identify the writer: its two captured attribute listings were identical, and an
observer side effect or an already pending OS operation could not be excluded.
No unsafe download completed. The signed build is preserved and will not be
published.

The reviewed correction retains the original ctime-inclusive comparator. Only a
valid clean scan, exact content hash and otherwise identical descriptor/path
metadata can request one fresh snapshot and full scan. A saved baseline is checked
before the retry and again before its helper starts. A second metadata change,
content or identity change, non-clean verdict, cancellation or deadline expiry
cannot release the download. Both attempts share the original time budget and
queue slot, and every retry snapshot must match the first snapshot's SHA-256
even when Chromium supplies no hash of its own.

The actual extracted native retry policy passed 13 functional scenarios, including
a same-size content rewrite with its modification time restored and a write
between the first post-scan hash and metadata read. The unchanged
strict-comparator check and 47 existing native/source/receipt regressions also
passed. The new runtime fault-injection wrapper still requires two actual scanner
attempts before completion after one QA metadata change, and cancellation without
a third scan after two changes. The replacement build `202610210001` now has the actual completed receipt
recorded above. Its new candidate runtime and signed-stage fault-injection checks
are separate: the new 19-group candidate run passed, while signed-stage fault
injection remains pending. The earlier candidate result is preserved as history.
